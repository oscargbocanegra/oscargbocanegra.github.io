Work With Me

Start with the problem, not a sales form.

A low-friction way to discuss an AI or data initiative that needs senior technical direction and delivery support.

Discuss a Project

Share only enough context to start a technical conversation. The site does not store form data or promise a response time.

What to send

Your name, professional email, a short problem or initiative description, and any non-sensitive context that helps frame the conversation. Current state and constraints are useful when available.

What happens next

After submitting the form, your details are received directly for review.

  1. Review: consider the shared non-sensitive context.
  2. Clarify: align on the problem, constraints, and intended outcome.
  3. Define: identify a practical next step or engagement shape.
  4. Engage: decide whether there is a useful way to work together.

If you prefer not to use the form, use {{ site.email }} or message on LinkedIn (opens in a new tab).
